C1
What This Automation Does
Plain-language explanation of the Payroll Setup flow

When a client signs up for our Payroll service, a LOT needs to happen. Before automation, this was all manual. Let's see the difference:

❌ Before Automation (Manual)
  • ⏰ Sales rep moves deal to "Signed" in CRM
  • ⏰ Sales rep sends email to onboarding: "New client!"
  • ⏰ Onboarding manually creates customer in Books
  • ⏰ Onboarding manually creates project in Projects
  • ⏰ Onboarding manually creates ticket in Desk
  • ⏰ Someone remembers to email the client welcome info
  • ⏰ Someone updates deal stage to "Onboarding"
  • ⚠️ Total time: 20-30 minutes per client
  • ⚠️ Error risk: High (forgotten steps, typos)
✅ After Automation
  • ⚡ Sales rep moves deal to "Signed" in CRM
  • 🤖 Everything else happens automatically:
  • ✓ Customer created in Books (instant)
  • ✓ Project created from template (instant)
  • ✓ Support ticket created and assigned (instant)
  • ✓ Welcome email sent to client (instant)
  • ✓ WhatsApp notification sent (instant)
  • ✓ Deal moves to "Onboarding" (instant)
  • 🎉 Total time: 5 seconds
  • 🎉 Error risk: Near zero
📝 What information do we collect?

The client fills out a form with their payroll details. This data flows through the entire system:

  • Company name and EIN (for tax filing)
  • Pay frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
  • State of registration (affects tax calculations)
  • Employee count (determines pricing tier)
  • Bank details for direct deposits
  • Contact info for communication
  • Start date and billing plan
📬 What does the client receive?

Immediately after signing:

  • Welcome email — confirms their signup, explains next steps, provides contact info
  • WhatsApp message — quick confirmation they can reply to with questions
👥 What does the internal team see?

The moment a deal is signed:

  • In CRM: Deal appears in "Onboarding" stage
  • In Projects: New project with all setup tasks listed
  • In Desk: New ticket assigned to onboarding team
  • In Books: Customer ready for invoicing

C5
CRM Blueprint — Advancing the Stage
Controlling when deals can move between stages
🗺️ What is a Blueprint?

A Blueprint is Zoho CRM's rule engine for stage transitions. It enforces that certain conditions are met before a deal can move forward — for example, requiring the client's EIN to be filled in before the deal advances to "Onboarding."

1
Access Blueprint in CRM
Zoho CRM→Setup ⚙️→Process Management→Blueprint

Click + Create Blueprint and select the Deals module.

2
Create the "Signed → Onboarding" transition

In the Blueprint canvas, drag from the Signed state to the Onboarding state to create a transition arrow. Name it: "Begin Onboarding"

💡 Each arrow in the Blueprint canvas is a transition. You control who can trigger it and what conditions must be met first.
3
Set required fields before the transition can happen

Click the transition arrow → go to "Before" tab → add these required fields:

  • EIN / Tax ID — must be filled
  • Pay Frequency — must be selected
  • Employee Count — must be filled
  • State — must be selected
⚠️ If these fields are empty, CRM will not allow the stage to advance. The team member will see a validation error prompting them to fill in the missing data.
4
Set who can trigger this transition

In the transition settings → "Who can perform this transition" → select the relevant roles (e.g., Sales, Admin). Save and activate the Blueprint.

Confirm records in Books and Projects

Spot-check 2–3 new payroll clients per week. Confirm their customer record in Books and their project in Projects were created correctly with all the right fields.
